Connect your WhatsApp to Freca

This links your WhatsApp Business app number to Freca. It keeps working on your phone, and can also reply when you are busy.

  1. Tap Connect, log in with Facebook.
  2. Choose Connect your existing WhatsApp Business app.
  3. Enter your number, then paste the code you receive in WhatsApp Business.

Use WhatsApp Business 2.24.17 or later, and keep the phone with you.
