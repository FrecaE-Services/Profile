    Our story

    Rooted in Ugandan dairy

    Too GooD Yogurt® is made by Mujuni Ventures Ltd, a Ugandan dairy company producing quality, affordable dairy for families across the country. We work hand in hand with more than 2,500 smallholder farmers in 14 dairy groups, turning their fresh milk into yogurt that nourishes communities and strengthens rural livelihoods.

    Our cows are raised on a plant-based diet and never given artificial growth hormones, so every cup is wholesome, nutritious and satisfying, with calcium and protein to keep you going through the day.
