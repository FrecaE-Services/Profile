For

Victoria

Words can’t explain how much I long to hear from you sometimes—to talk to you, to hear your voice, to tell you the little things that happen in my day.

Some days, the loneliness becomes almost unbearable. Not because I have no one around me, but because somehow, you’re the one person I wish I could share things with. And then reality reminds me that you aren’t really there.

What stings even more is feeling you slowly distance yourself from me. I notice it, even when I pretend I don’t.

Most of the time, I never wanted much from you. Sometimes, all I needed was a little warmth, a kind response, a simple feeling that talking to me wasn’t a burden. I’ve done some things simply because I wanted to see you happy, and it stings when those same things are met with annoyance—as though my intentions were something wrong.

Deep down, I’m just a lonely person. I’m introverted. I don’t have many people I can truly open up to, and even fewer people I can trust with the parts of my life I rarely show anyone.

For a while, I thought maybe that person could be you.

I wanted you to be the person I could call when my mind was heavy. The person I could tell the things I couldn’t tell everyone else. The person whose presence could make an ordinary day feel a little less empty.

But I’m slowly accepting that you are currently not that person.

And maybe that is the part that stings the most—not losing something I had, but realizing that something I quietly hoped for may never exist.

I’ve gotten used to being alone.

I’ve learned how to carry the silence.

I’ve learned how to miss someone without saying it.

But sometimes, even after getting used to loneliness, it still stings.

Because I really didn’t want much from you.

Maybe I just wanted a little place in your world.

And perhaps, even that little bit…

was already too much to ask.
